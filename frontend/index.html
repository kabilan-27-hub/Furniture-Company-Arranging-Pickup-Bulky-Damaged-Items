Ethics & Responsible Operations Policy

Driver & Workforce Well-being

Maximum shift limits (9 hours) and mandatory rest periods are enforced as hard constraints. Route optimization never forces dangerous driving speeds or excessive driver overtime.

Vehicle Loading Safety

Both volume ($m^3$) and weight ($kg$) payloads are monitored dynamically at every stop along the route timeline to avoid dangerous vehicle overloading.

Auditability & Privacy

Every constraint override requires explicit dispatcher identity, timestamp, and justification logging. Customer location data is stored strictly for routing optimization.
